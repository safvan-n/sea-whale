Our Heritage

The Sea Whale Transport Narrative

Founded in 2018 with a vision to connect local sustainable fishing vessels to premium culinary markets, Sea Whale Transport has grown into India's leading specialized cold-chain logistics brand. We don't just ship food; we preserve the natural textures, rich oils, and absolute flavor profiles of deep sea catches.

By blending IoT-monitored refrigerated trucks, nitrogen insulated shipping packs, and zero-chemical handling protocols, we create an unbroken bridge of cold trust from ocean tides directly to your gourmet creations.

Our Core Mission

To deliver absolute freshness and uncompromised food quality using advanced cold chain technology, while actively promoting eco-friendly, sustainable fishing practices across all maritime coordinates.

Our Global Vision

To set the global standard for marine protein transport, ensuring zero food spoilage and introducing premium sub-zero culinary experiences to epicures around the country.

Cold Chain Infrastructure

Active Nitrogen Cooling & IoT Fleet Controls

Every transport reefer in our Sea Whale fleet is equipped with dual temperature sensors linked to a live cloud tracker. If temperatures deviate by even 0.5°C, our central operations center triggers an automated backup coolant override. Our custom Nitrogen insulated home delivery pods maintain an ice-cold lock without using melting water ice, ensuring your fish fillets remain dry, pristine, and perfectly chilled.
